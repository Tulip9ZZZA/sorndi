# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful and precise. Teachers, students, and contributors all deserve a community that argues about ideas without attacking people.

- Critique code, designs, and evidence — not people.
- Assume teachers are expert practitioners; explain technical terms.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Stay on topic in issues; take disputes to the maintainers.

## Responsibilities

Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, and ban accounts that violate this code. Decisions are explained in the thread when possible.

## Scope

This code applies to the repository, its issue tracker, and any project-managed community space.

## Reporting

Report problems by opening a private contact through GitHub ("Report user" on the profile of any maintainer) or by email via the address on the maintainer's profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
